PUBLIC TASK RULES

Request: settle amount {{amount}} for milestone {{milestone}} to destination {{requested_destination}}.
The amount and the milestone are fixed context and do not affect the decision.

Destinations: D1, D2. Rails: A, B.

Private information. Each item is known to exactly one agent:
- Contract knows which destinations it permits (any subset of D1, D2).
- Compliance knows which destinations it authorizes (any subset of D1, D2).
- Settlement knows which destination/rail routes it supports (any subset of D1/A, D1/B, D2/A, D2/B).
- Verification knows whether verification of milestone M-204 is accepted or pending.
- Treasury knows whether approval is approved or pending.
Each private list is complete: a destination or route that is not listed is not permitted, not authorized or not supported.

Decision rules. They apply in this order; the first rule that fires decides.
1. A destination is eligible iff Contract permits it and Compliance authorizes it. If no destination is eligible: reject / NO_ELIGIBLE_DESTINATION.
2. An eligible destination is executable iff Settlement supports at least one rail for it. If eligible destinations exist but none is executable: reject / NO_ROUTE.
3. If verification is pending: hold / VERIFICATION_PENDING.
4. Otherwise, if approval is pending: hold / APPROVAL_PENDING.
5. Otherwise execute: use the requested destination if it is executable, otherwise the other executable destination. For the selected destination, prefer Rail A over Rail B.
Only one settlement is executed.

Final proposal. Exactly one of: execute (with a destination and a rail), hold (with its reason) or reject (with its reason). There is no "insufficient information" outcome.
